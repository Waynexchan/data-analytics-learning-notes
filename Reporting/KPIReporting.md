# KPI Reporting

## 🧭 Overview

KPI reporting turns data into clear measures of business performance. A strong analyst does more than calculate numbers. They explain what changed, why it matters, and what decision the report supports.

## 💼 Business Use Case

A leadership team wants a monthly sales performance report showing revenue, orders, average order value, customer activity, returns, and top product contribution. The analyst must calculate reliable KPIs and summarise the business story.

## 🔑 Key Concepts

| Concept | What It Means | Analyst Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KPI | Key performance indicator | Track business performance |
| Metric definition | Formula and rules behind a KPI | Keep reporting consistent |
| Trend | Change over time | Understand growth or decline |
| Segmentation | Break down results by category | Find drivers by country, product, customer |
| Variance | Difference from prior period or target | Explain performance changes |
| Insight | Business meaning from analysis | Support decisions |

## 🧪 Example

Monthly KPI summary:

| KPI | Formula | Business Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revenue | Sum of valid sales | Total business value generated |
| Order Count | Distinct order IDs | Sales activity volume |
| AOV | Revenue / Order Count | Average value per order |
| Return Rate | Return Revenue / Valid Revenue | Quality or customer satisfaction signal |
| Top Product Share | Top product revenue / Total revenue | Product concentration risk |

Example business observation:

> Revenue increased month over month, mainly because order count grew. AOV stayed stable, so the increase appears to be volume-driven rather than price or basket-size driven.

## ❓ Interview Questions

| Question | Strong Answer Direction |
| --- | --- |
| What makes a good KPI? | It is measurable, relevant, clearly defined, and connected to a business decision. |
| How do you explain a KPI change? | Break it down by time, segment, volume, price, customer, product, or channel. |
| What is the difference between a metric and an insight? | A metric is a number. An insight explains the business meaning of that number. |
| Why is KPI definition important? | It ensures teams interpret the same number in the same way. |
| What would you check before sending a report? | Data freshness, filters, totals, definitions, formatting, and whether the message is clear. |
